The list is in the public domain (CC0 1.0). Section colours follow the false-colour scales of SDO's AIA channels. The disc at the top is an idealised surface flux transport model: bipolar regions emerge in the activity belt with Hale's and Joy's laws, their flux spreads and is carried poleward, and the trailing flux reverses the polar field once every cycle. Also available as JSON and plain text. Maintained by Soumyaranjan Dash.
